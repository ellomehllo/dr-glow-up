# Tool study: what each tool really does off its target

Every treatment tool is a real object. Used on something other than what it is for, it
does what that object would do there. The game builds all of it (js/world.js). Marks on
the room are put right at the start of every level; marks on a patient stay on that
patient until they leave.

Places a tool can land: the patient's **hair**, **skin**, **nose**, **mouth**, **eyes**
and **clothes**; the room's **doorway glass**, **waiting customers** (once the glass is
gone), **clock**, **exam lamp**, **plus sign**, **certificate**, **shelf bottles**,
**cabinet**, **curtains**, **wall** and **floor**; the floating **soap bubbles**.

Every tool in an **eye** hurts (red, watering, costs aura). Every pointed or pinching tool
pops a **bubble** it touches.

| Tool | Real object | Patient (off target) | Room |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Wash | soapy sponge | hair: soap foam sits in it; mouth: soap taste, "BLEH"; clothes: a wet patch | glass and walls: wipes marks away, squeaky clean; customers: splashed; makes new bubbles as it goes |
| Pop pimples | two fingers | skin: a pinch; nose: a honk; hair: a tug; clothes: a poke, "HEY!" | glass: a knock the customers turn to; clock: nudged crooked; bottles: flicked off the shelf; customers: poked |
| Sculpt jaw | jade roller | skin: cool and soothing; hair: rolls it into a tangle; clothes: lint-rolled clean | glass: a smudge; customers: rolled, they giggle |
| Sit up | posture (no object) | nothing to touch | nothing to touch |
| Paint hair | hair-dye brush | skin: stray paint (already); clothes: a paint streak | glass, walls, objects, floor: paint strokes in the dye colour; customers: painted, annoyed |
| Heal scars | gel tube | skin: a soothing dab; hair: slicked with shiny gel; mouth: "YUCK"; clothes: a gel blob | glass and walls: a gel blob that runs; customers: gelled, "EWW" |
| Glow | powder puff | hair: dusted white; clothes: powdered | glass and walls: a powder print, a puff cloud; customers: sneeze |
| Nose job | mallet | skin: a bruise; head: a lump; mouth: knocks a tooth out; clothes: "OOF" | glass: cracks, then shatters; clock: knocked crooked and stops; lamp: bulb smashed, light out; bottles: knocked off; certificate: crooked; walls and cabinet: a dent; customers: bonked, they run |
| Dark circles | cucumber slice | skin: cool dab; mouth: they eat it; hair: the slice stays there; clothes: a wet green spot | glass: the slice sticks and slides down; walls: it sticks; customers: they take it and eat it |
| Laser | laser pen | hair: burnt bald; skin: permanent burn; clothes: a burnt hole | glass: reflects; bubbles: pop; customers (no glass): hurt, they run; walls and objects: scorch marks; curtains: catch, smoke, a burnt hole; lamp: bulb pops |
| Whiten teeth | bleaching lamp | skin: sunburn; hair: a warm shine; clothes: a bleached pale patch | glass: glare that dazzles the customers; customers: dazzled, squinting |
| Iron wrinkles | hot iron | skin: a burn; hair: straightened, it sizzles and shines; clothes: pressed crisp, press the same spot again and it scorches | glass: fogged with steam; curtains: pressed, then scorched; walls: a puff of steam; customers: too hot, they run |
| Tweezers | tweezers | hair: plucked bald; brows: thinner; skin: a pinch; clothes: pulls a loose thread | curtains: pulls a thread; customers: a hair plucked, "OW!" |
| Brush teeth | toothbrush and paste | skin: a toothpaste smear; hair: paste in it; clothes: a paste stain | glass and walls: toothpaste smears; customers: pasted |
| Fill cavities | dentist's drill | skin: a graze; hair: wound into a tangle; clothes: drills a hole | glass: a drilled hole with cracks (counts toward breaking it); walls, cabinet: a hole and dust; clock: stops; lamp: smashed; customers: they run |
| Pull bad tooth | pliers | skin: a pinch; nose: twisted, honk; hair: yanks a bald patch; clothes: tugs a thread | glass: a scratch; bottles: picked off and dropped; clock: bends the hand, it stops; customers: tugged, "OW!" |
