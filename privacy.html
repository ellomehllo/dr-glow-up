Dr. Glow Up

A skin clinic game by corkless.io

Privacy

Dr. Glow Up does not collect, sell or share any personal information.

  • No accounts, sign-ins, ads, analytics or tracking.
  • Your level progress is saved only on your own device. Deleting the app (or clearing this site's data in your browser) removes it.
  • The iOS app works fully offline and sends nothing over the internet.
  • The web version loads its font from Google Fonts, which receives the usual request details (such as your IP address) when the page loads.

This applies to everyone who plays, including children.

Support

Found a bug or have a question? Open an issue on GitHub and we'll get back to you: github.com/ellomehllo/dr-glow-up/issues

Tips: press and hold to start each treatment and let go on the star. Too early or too late costs points.
