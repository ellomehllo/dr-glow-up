Dr. Glow Up

A skin clinic game by corkless.io

Privacy

Dr. Glow Up does not sell or share your information, and it has no ads, analytics or tracking. There are no email or password accounts.

  • Your level progress is saved on your own device. Deleting the app (or clearing this site's data in your browser) removes it.
  • Progress backup. On the website and in the iPhone and Mac apps, once you finish your first level the game backs up your progress (which levels are unlocked and your best score on each) to our server, run on Supabase. It is stored under a random player ID kept on your device: no email, phone number or real name.
  • Multiplayer. If you open multiplayer, the same random player ID is used and our server also keeps:
    • the username you pick (shown as @name to the people you play with; please don't use your real name),
    • the characters you make and how they look after other players work on them, so you can see your character's history,
    • the matches and rooms you join, the room codes, and the reactions you and others give (love it, funny or ruined).
    During a turn your character's look is sent live to the other players in your game. There is no chat and no free text other than usernames, which are checked against a blocklist. Skipping a player means you won't be paired with them again.
  • Deleting the app or clearing this site's data disconnects you from your player ID. To have your backup and multiplayer data deleted, open an issue (link below) with your @username and we'll remove it.
  • The website is served by Cloudflare and loads its font from Google Fonts. Cloudflare, Google Fonts and Supabase receive the usual request details (such as your IP address) when the game talks to them.

This applies to everyone who plays, including children. Children should play multiplayer with a parent's okay and choose a username that isn't their real name.

Support

Found a bug or have a question? Open an issue on GitHub and we'll get back to you: github.com/ellomehllo/dr-glow-up/issues

Tips: press and hold to start each treatment and let go on the star. Too early or too late costs points.
